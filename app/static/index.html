Prototype · Suisse Meets challenge B

Invite a colleague

The admin can only invite. The person opens their own link, reads the consent text and records their voice themself — nobody can give consent on someone else's behalf.

Voice profiles

Only a 192-number voice fingerprint is stored (encrypted with this customer's own key). The recording itself is never kept.

Analyse a meeting recording

Diarization splits the audio by voice, then each anonymous speaker is compared with this customer's profiles only. Below the threshold the speaker stays unnamed — a wrong name is worse than no name.

Audit log
